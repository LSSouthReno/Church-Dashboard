Planning Center Scheduling
📱 How PCO Scheduling Works for Volunteers

The following standard PCO process will be included in the guide automatically. Add any team-specific details below.

  • 1. You'll receive an invite to Planning Center via email — accept it to create your account.
  • 2. Download Church Center (iOS / Android) and sign in. This is your main hub for your schedule.
  • 3. When you're scheduled, you'll get a notification + email asking you to Accept or Decline.
  • 4. Accept or Decline within 48 hours so we can fill any gaps quickly.
  • 5. After accepting, your full plan (notes, song list, service order) is visible in the Church Center or PCO Services app.
📅 How to Block Out Dates in Church Center

This step-by-step will be included in the guide for volunteers:

  • 1. Open the Church Center app on your phone.
  • 2. Tap your profile picture (top right corner).
  • 3. Tap "Serving" → then "Block Out Dates."
  • 4. Select the dates you're unavailable and tap Save.
  • 5. The scheduling team will automatically see these when building the schedule.
